Хөнгөлөх дүн нь тухайн татварын жилийн татвар ногдох орлогын 1 хувь хүртэл хэмжээнээс хэтрэхгүй байна. Art. 22.9 — Investment in fixed assets subject to depreciation, financial support and donations granted to unrelated parties for the purpose of supporting socially responsible activities unrelated to the taxpayer's income-generating activities shall be deducted from corporate income tax assessed pursuant to Art. 20.1. The deduction shall not exceed 1% of taxable income for the relevant tax year.
